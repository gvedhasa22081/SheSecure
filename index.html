Digital Privacy Education

🔑 Password Protection

Use strong, unique passwords for every online account. Enable two-factor authentication wherever possible.

👤 Social Media Privacy

Review your profile visibility, location sharing, friend lists and personal information.

⚠ Online Harassment

Preserve relevant messages, screenshots, URLs and dates. Block and report abusive accounts when safe to do so.

🎣 Phishing Awareness

Avoid suspicious links and unexpected attachments. Never share OTPs or account passwords.

🔒 Evidence Protection

Preserve original evidence and keep secure backups. Avoid publicly sharing sensitive information.

📍 Location Privacy

Disable unnecessary location permissions and avoid publicly sharing your live location.

About SheSecure

SheSecure is an academic digital privacy and online harassment evidence management project. This version stores data locally in your browser. Incident text is not encrypted. Evidence files are encrypted. Clearing browser data may permanently remove records. Use demonstration data rather than real sensitive evidence.
