Privacy education

Simple habits can make your digital life safer.

Protect your accounts

Use unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication wherever available. Never share one-time passwords.

Review privacy settings

Limit who can see your posts, profile information, phone number and location. Review these settings regularly.

Preserve evidence safely

When documenting harassment, preserve original messages, dates and account details where safe. Avoid editing original evidence files.

Get help and report

In India, you can visit the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
